                    About

                    From the epic to the evidence

                    Kurukshetra is a Mini Research & Innovation Project, B.Tech II Year, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Chennai. Theme: Strategic Lessons from the Mahabharata.

                    1. Mahabharata. Abhimanyu breaks into the Chakravyuha. He knows only half of what he needs, his allies are held back at the breach, and the formation keeps turning.
                    2. Strategic insight. Decisions made with partial, changing information, by people who each know something different.
                    3. Contemporary problem. Teams act on incomplete or stale information, and coordination failures turn good individual choices into bad outcomes.
                    4. The game. A human and autonomous agents each hold a different slice of the truth. Information and coordination are experimental conditions.
                    5. Evidence. Every move is logged against what you believed and what was true, so information errors and decision errors can be told apart.

                    Sessions are stored only in this browser. Export them from the research dashboard.
